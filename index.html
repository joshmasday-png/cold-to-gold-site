About us

Automation built around how your business already works.

C2G (Cold to Gold) Studios is an AI automation studio based in Singapore. We build the systems that sit between a company's leads, customers and tools, so enquiries get answered, follow-ups happen and records stay accurate without anyone chasing them.

  • What we build

    Workflows, AI voice agents, customer support assistants and multi-agent systems. Whether it's a single follow-up flow or a system that runs across sales, support and billing, every build is shaped around your operations.

  • How we work

    We start with the process that costs your team the most time, build it on your own accounts and test it against real examples before it goes live. You receive full documentation and full ownership.

  • What we stand for

    AI that sticks to the facts. Our systems answer from your own data, check what they say before they say it, and leave the decisions that matter to a person. If something shouldn't be automated, we'll tell you.

Services

What we build

Each engagement starts with one job your team does by hand and ends with a system that does it for them.

01

WhatsApp replies and follow-up

New enquiries receive an answer straight away, a follow-up the next day if they haven't booked, and a booking link when they're ready.

02

AI voice agents

An assistant that calls new leads, asks the qualifying questions, retries when nobody picks up and records the outcome.

03

Lead pipelines

Website forms, ads and WhatsApp feed into one CRM, with a status, an owner and a next step for every lead.

04

E-commerce flows

Abandoned carts, post-purchase messages, review requests and restock alerts, handled automatically.

05

CRM and reporting sync

Your CRM updates itself as leads move through the pipeline, so the numbers are accurate without manual checks.

06

Agent systems

Multiple AI agents working on one task, each limited to its own data, with checks on what they say and human sign-off at the end.

We work with n8n, Make.com, OpenAI, MCP, Vapi, Twilio, WhatsApp Business API, Telegram, Airtable, Calendly, Shopify, PandaDoc and Google Workspace.

Process

From first call to live in about two weeks

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Audit

    A 20-minute call to map how leads and requests move through your business and identify the step that costs the most time.

  2. 2
    Week 1

    Build

    We connect your tools, write the AI logic and test it against real examples until it handles the edge cases.

  3. 3
    Week 2

    Handover

    The system goes live on your accounts with documentation your team can follow, and we remain available for support.
