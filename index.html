01  About us

About us

Cold to Gold is an AI automation studio based in Singapore. We build the systems that sit between a company's leads, customers and tools, so enquiries get answered, follow-ups happen and records stay accurate without anyone chasing them.

What we build

Workflows in n8n and Make.com, AI voice agents, customer support assistants and multi-agent systems, where several AI agents work together on one task.

Whether it's a single follow-up flow or a system that runs across sales, support and billing, every build is shaped around how your business already works.

How we work

We start with the one process that costs your team the most time. We build it on your own accounts and test it against real examples before it goes live.

We design and build every system ourselves, from the prompts and workflow logic to the screens your team uses. You get full documentation and full ownership.

What we care about

AI that sticks to the facts. Our systems answer from your own data, check what they say before they say it, and leave the decisions that matter to a person.

If something shouldn't be automated, we'll tell you.

02  Services

What we build

Each project starts with one job your team does by hand and ends with a system that does it for them.

  1. WhatsApp replies and follow-up

    New enquiries get an answer straight away, a nudge the next day if they haven't booked, and a booking link when they're ready.

  2. AI voice agents

    An assistant that calls new leads, asks the qualifying questions, retries when nobody picks up and logs what happened. Built on VAPI and Twilio.

  3. Lead pipelines

    Website forms, ads and WhatsApp all land in one CRM with a status, an owner and a next step.

  4. E-commerce flows

    Abandoned carts, post-purchase messages, review requests and restock alerts.

  5. CRM and reporting sync

    Airtable or your CRM updates itself as leads move, so the numbers are right without anyone checking.

  6. Agent systems

    Several AI agents working on one task, each limited to its own data, with checks on what they say and a person signing off at the end.

Tools we use: n8n, Make.com, OpenAI, MCP, VAPI, Twilio, WhatsApp Business API, Telegram, Airtable, Calendly, Shopify, PandaDoc, Google Sheets.

03  Process

From first call to live in about two weeks

  1. Day 1

    Audit

    A 20-minute call. We go through how leads and requests move through your business today and pick the step that costs you the most time.

  2. Week 1

    Build

    We connect your tools, write the AI logic and run it against real examples until it handles the awkward cases.

  3. Week 2

    Hand over

    It goes live on your accounts with documentation your team can follow. We stay on for support and whatever you want automated next.
