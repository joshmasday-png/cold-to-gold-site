About the studio

Automation that fits how your business already works.

C2G (Cold to Gold) Studios is a small automation studio in Singapore. We connect your leads, customers and tools so enquiries get answered, follow-ups happen and records stay accurate, without anyone having to chase them.

  1. Start where the time goes

    We begin with the one task that costs your team the most hours, and fix that first.

  2. Stick to the facts

    Our AI answers from your own information, checks itself before it speaks and leaves the important decisions to a person.

  3. You own everything

    Every system runs on your own accounts, with clear documentation. If something shouldn't be automated, we'll tell you.

Services

What we build

Every project starts with one job your team does by hand, and ends with a system that does it for them.

  1. 01

    WhatsApp replies and follow-up

    Every new enquiry gets an answer straight away, a reminder the next day if they haven't booked, and a booking link when they're ready.

  2. 02

    AI phone assistants

    An assistant that calls new leads, asks your questions, tries again when nobody picks up and records how each call went.

  3. 03

    Lead tracking

    Website forms, ads and WhatsApp all feed into one list, with a status, an owner and a next step for every lead.

  4. 04

    Online store messages

    Abandoned cart reminders, thank-you messages, review requests and back-in-stock alerts, sent automatically.

  5. 05

    Records that update themselves

    Your customer records and reports stay up to date as leads move along, so the numbers are right without manual checking.

  6. 06

    Teams of AI assistants

    Several AI assistants sharing one larger task, each limited to its own information, with checks on what they say and a person signing off at the end.

Process

From first call to live in about two weeks

No long discovery phase and no endless back-and-forth. One clear goal, built and tested on your own accounts.

  1. Day 1

    Review

    A 20-minute call to understand how enquiries move through your business and find the step that costs the most time.

  2. Week 1

    Build

    We connect your tools, set up the AI and test it on real examples until it handles the awkward cases.

  3. Week 2

    Handover

    It goes live on your accounts with simple instructions for your team, and we stay on hand for support.
